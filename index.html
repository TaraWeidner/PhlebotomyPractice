Beginner Reference

Tube Cheat Sheet

Gold / Tiger SST

Serum separator tube. Commonly used for chemistry, thyroid, hormones, lipids, vitamins, and many serologies.

Light Blue

Sodium citrate. Commonly used for coagulation testing such as PT/INR. Correct fill is especially important.

Lavender

EDTA whole blood. Commonly used for CBC/CBC with differential and hemoglobin A1c.

Remember: This is a learning shortcut, not a universal specimen manual. Verify the exact test in the current lab directory before a real collection.
