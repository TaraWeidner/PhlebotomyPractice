Tube Reference

Tube Cheat Sheet

Plain Red

No anticoagulant. Produces serum after clotting. If separated serum is required, allow clotting, centrifuge, then transfer serum away from cells as directed.

Light Blue

Sodium citrate. Commonly used for coagulation testing such as PT/INR. Correct fill is especially important.

Gold / Tiger SST

Clot activator plus gel barrier. Produces serum. Allow complete clotting before centrifugation; acceptable assays may be transported in the correctly spun tube with an intact barrier.

Green

Lithium or sodium heparin. Can be whole blood or plasma depending on the assay. If plasma is required, centrifuge/separate within the test-specific window.

Lavender

K2EDTA. Commonly used as whole blood for CBC/CBC with differential and A1c. EDTA plasma is also used for some assays.

Pink

K2EDTA cross-match tube. Commonly associated with blood-bank workflows, but the receiving facility determines whether Pink, Lavender, or another container is acceptable.

Gray

Sodium fluoride with an anticoagulant. Used for selected glucose/lactate workflows. Processing and temperature requirements are highly test-specific.

Advanced rule: Never decide whether to spin a tube from cap color alone. Read the requested specimen: serum, plasma, or whole blood—then follow the current test-directory and manufacturer instructions.
